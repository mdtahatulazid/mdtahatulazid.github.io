THE PERSON BEHIND THE RESEARCH

EPISODE 01 / THE ORIGIN STORY

Curious about cities.
Committed to people.

Working at the intersection of urban systems, environmental change and the communities they shape.

An urban and regional planning graduate of RUET and an MSc student in Environmental Science and Management at Bangladesh University of Professionals, Tahatul connects spatial analysis with practical planning. His experience spans climate and disaster risk studies, municipal preparedness, community engagement, geospatial mapping and circular-economy project development.

Across academic research and professional assignments, a common thread runs through the work: use evidence to understand a place, listen to the people who live there, and develop more resilient paths forward.
